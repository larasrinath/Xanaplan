XANAPLAN · EARLY PROTOTYPE

Give your columns room.

Open a classic Anaplan module with line items across the columns.

  • Drag the right edge of a header to widen or narrow it.
  • Double-click the edge to fit the header and rendered cells.
  • Keyboard: tab to an edge, use arrows to resize or Enter to fit.

Uses Anaplan’s own width settings. The settings dialog may appear briefly. Saving the view follows Anaplan’s normal behavior.

Version 0.1 supports English classic module grids with one header level. No account, backend, or data uploads.
